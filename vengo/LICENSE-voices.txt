VenGo's voice clips (the sounds folder)
=======================================

The spoken alerts in sounds\ were made with free text-to-speech voices, run offline with sherpa-onnx
(https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx, Apache License 2.0). The clips are the voices' output; no voice model is
included with VenGo.

sounds\scot_f   Scottish female: the Piper voice "alba" (en_GB-alba-medium), trained on a Scottish English
                speech corpus from the University of Edinburgh (https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3270),
                licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

sounds\gb_m     British male: Kokoro-82M by hexgrad (https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), voice "bm_george",
                Apache License 2.0.

sounds\ja_f     Japanese female: Kokoro-82M by hexgrad, voice "jf_alpha", Apache License 2.0.
