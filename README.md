VenGo

VenGo is a VanaCore addon for CatsEyeXI that puts the whole Goblin Ventures board in one window, styled after YunaUI, and gets you to a VNM in one click.

Features

Live ventures board: reads the server’s venture data as it arrives, so zones and progress are always current. Shows ACE or CW zones, Pool A, Pool B, or both together, sorted with 100% at the top.
VNM details: each venture shows its NM, grid square and equipment drops. Hover an NM for notes, element and crest. The HVNM card shows its progress and loot.
One-click GO: stand at a Home Point, Survival Guide or Unity NPC and press GO. VenGo picks the warp of that kind that lands closest to the VNM and warps you there through Uberwarp.
Press GO anywhere else and it waits, then warps the moment you walk up to a warp NPC.
If the closest warp isn’t unlocked, “Try next closest” picks the next one.
Alzadaal Undersea Ruins VNMs warp to Whitegate.
VNM alerts: when a venture hits 100%, or Populox calls a spawn in Unity chat, a glowing banner pops up with the NM, zone, a countdown to the pop, and its own GO button. A sound plays at the alert and again one minute before the pop. There’s an optional chat heads-up at 90%.
HELM, Fishing and Dynamis ventures, each on its own tab.
YunaUI look: dark glass panel, purple accents, AvantGarde-style font. The window is resizable and has a see-through mode. It minimizes to a small VenGo tag that pulses during an alert.
VenGo never moves your character; it only uses warps you’ve already unlocked.

Requirements

The VanaCore client (the CatsEyeXI launcher).
The Uberwarp plugin, for GO warps.

Install

Unzip so you have VanaCore\addons\vanacore\vengo\vengo.lua.
In game, type /addon load vengo vanacore, and add that line to your startup script.
Type /vengo to open or close the window.
