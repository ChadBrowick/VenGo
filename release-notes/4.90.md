# VenGo 4.90

- **New style: AETHER** (Settings > Colours > Style), after ChadcoreMenu's Aether look:
  - dark blue panels with round corners in a thin line of pale light;
  - a leaning title bar with the page's name in serif capitals, the Japanese under it, and its icon in a box at
    the end, with a spark running round both;
  - leaning menu rows in italic serif; the one you're on is outlined in light with a double chevron;
  - round buttons in the same light, and the VNM alert pop-up in it too (gold for a starred VNM);
  - glints and motes of light twinkling round the window.
- Choosing Aether switches the accent from Red to Blue the first time; any accent can be picked afterwards.
- Everything it needs is in VenGo's own folder: nothing of ChadcoreMenu's is used.
