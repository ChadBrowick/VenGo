# VenGo 4.67

## Spark vendor (new tab, above Settings, `/vengo sparks`)

- Shows your Sparks of Eminence, what they're worth in gil as Acheron Shields, how many shields you can buy (sparks,
  the week's limit and bag space) and how many are already in your bag.
- **BUY**: stand by a Sparks NPC (Isakoth, Rolandienne, Fhelm Jobeizat, Eternal Flame), pick how many (− / + / MAX)
  and press BUY. VenGo talks to the NPC with the menu kept shut, buys one shield at a time (2,755 sparks each), checks
  the sparks the server says are left after each one, and closes the menu.
- **SELL**: open any NPC shop, then press SELL. Each Acheron Shield in your bag is valued by the shop and sold, one at
  a time (27,550 gil each). Equipped shields are left alone.

## Neon style

- New style in Settings > Colours: **NEON**, ChadcoreMenu 3.57's Neon look. It has dark slabs in a glowing line of
  the accent, italic serif titles with an icon box and the Japanese under them, and a spark running round the title.
  The menu rows lean, with a honeycomb on the one you're on, and buttons and section names are in the same style.
  The VNM pop-up uses it too, gold for a starred VNM.
- Pick one of 7 accents (Red, Crimson, Blue, Gold, Pink, Green, Purple) and a slab colour.
- Phantom is unchanged, and Spark vendor has its own picture title there.

## Japanese

- The new tab, its messages and the Neon option are translated.
