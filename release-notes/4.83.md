# VenGo 4.83

## Window size

- New **-** and **+** at the top of the window, left of the see-through button: the whole window smaller or bigger,
  10% a press, from 50% to 300%. Point at either to see the size it is at.
- Everything in the window scales together: text, buttons, rows, pictures and tooltips. The window keeps its
  top-right corner where it is, so the buttons stay under the pointer as you press.
- The size is saved with your settings and presets. At 100% nothing changes from before.
- Only the main window scales. The logo has its own size; the venture bar, the pop-up and the dailies tracker stay
  as they are.

## No icon

- Settings > Window > Icon has a third choice, **NO ICON**: shrinking VenGo then leaves nothing on screen. Type
  `/vengo` to open it again (VenGo says so in chat when it hides). Alerts still pop up.
