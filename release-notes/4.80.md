# VenGo 4.80

- GPS styles: **NEON BANDS** and **SOLID NEON** are removed. The four left are LINES, RUNES, LIGHT and AETHER, now
  on one row in Settings > Trail to the VNM.
- If you had one of the removed styles picked (SOLID NEON was the default), your GPS now shows as LINES until you
  pick another. Saved presets with a removed style do the same.
