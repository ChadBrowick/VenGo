**Download:** VenGo-v4.54.zip below (under Assets).

- New **Voice** choice in Settings > VNM Alerts: a Windows speech voice says the VNM and its zone when it's announced ("V N M incoming! Puffer Pugil, in Buburimu Peninsula.") and again a minute before it pops.
  - **Scottish female, Australian female, British male, Japanese female** (the Japanese one speaks Japanese), or Off.
  - Uses the voices Windows has, through PowerShell in the background. Add one in Windows: Settings > Time & language > Speech > Add voices (English (Australia), English (United Kingdom), Japanese), then press FIND VOICES.
  - The settings show which Windows voice will speak. Windows has no Scottish voice, so that one uses the closest British female voice.
  - TEST VOICE tries it; it plays alongside the alert sound, and works even with the pop-up or chat lines turned off.

Unzip so you have `VanaCore\addons\ashita\vengo\vengo.lua` (or `addons\vanacore\vengo`), then `/addon reload vengo`. Or press INSTALL in Settings > Updates.
