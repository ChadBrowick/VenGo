# VenGo 5.03

- **Venture bar size**: the bar VenGo minimizes to can be made smaller or bigger, 60% to 200%, 10% a press:
  - with the **- / +** on the bar itself (beside its Open button);
  - or with **Bar size** in Settings > Window (RESET puts it back to 100%).
- Everything on the bar scales together (text, icons, spacing), and the size is remembered.
