# VenGo 4.93

Everything from 4.92 in a new release:

- **LB + Y opens VenGo with the controller** (L1 + Triangle on a PlayStation pad), and pressing it again closes it.
  - While VenGo has the controller, **LB / RB** change to the menu before / after.
  - **B** closes VenGo and gives the controller back (while you're changing a slider or a box, B cancels that first).
  - **Back / Select** still gives the controller back and leaves VenGo open.
  - Switch it off with "LB + Y opens VenGo" in Settings > Controller.
- **The Chadbrowick logo** is on the SPECIAL THANKS page, on the right, with the names beside it (4.91).
- **AETHER style** in Settings > Colours > Style, after ChadcoreMenu's Aether look (4.90).
