**Download:** VenGo-v4.58.zip below (under Assets).

- New **Phantom style** (Settings > Colours > Style): red, black and white, slanted, like ChadcoreMenu's Phantom style.
  - The panel is a slanted black slab edged in white, on a red one.
  - The page's name is in cut-out letters (heavy black letters edged white, the first bigger, one white-on-black) on red shards, with "VenGo" beside it.
  - The menu is dark slanted strips; the page you're on is a white slab with a red slash and a bobbing pointer.
  - Section names are white capitals on black slab tags.
  - A bold narrow font throughout.
  - Its own colour picks: **Accent** (Red, Crimson, Blue, Gold, Pink, Green, Purple), **Slab** (Black, Charcoal, Navy, Wine) and **Edge** (White, Cream, Silver, Black).
- **Classic** (VenGo's own look) is still the default. VenGo doesn't need ChadcoreMenu: the Phantom fonts come with it in `fonts` (Inter Black, SIL Open Font License; TeX Gyre Heros Cn Bold, GUST Font License).

Unzip so you have `VanaCore\addons\ashita\vengo\vengo.lua` (and its folders), then `/addon reload vengo`. Or press INSTALL in Settings > Updates.
