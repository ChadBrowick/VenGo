# VenGo 4.79

## Three new GPS styles

Settings > Trail to the VNM > GPS style has a second row, drawn on the ground itself:

- **RUNES**: rune circles every few yalms (two rings, rune marks that turn slowly, a four-point star), joined by a
  line, with a brightness running out along them. They stay put on the ground as you walk.
- **LIGHT**: one bright line from an orb at your feet, with a brighter length of it racing out to the VNM.
- **AETHER**: faint wisps weaving along the ground, motes of light drifting on, and a spirit light running the path
  with rings swirling under it.

All three end in a new marker at the VNM's spot: rings on the ground, beams of light rising from them, and two
chevrons pointing down. The name and distance sit above the chevrons. GPS colour, size, spacing, speed, reach and
glow apply to them; pick Blue for the look in the pictures they were made from.

## "Arrow" is now "GPS"

- Settings: the Arrows section is **GPS**, and Arrow style / size / colour are **GPS style / size / colour**. The
  tooltips, the lines under GO in the window and the chat lines say GPS too.
