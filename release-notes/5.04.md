# VenGo 5.04

- **The VNM pop-up keeps the most important VNM on top** when more than one is up at once:
  - the **Hard VNM** always shows over the rest;
  - a **starred VNM** (its zone or one of its pieces starred) always shows over one that isn't;
  - between two of the same kind, the newest shows.
- The others aren't lost: they wait, and come up in turn when the one showing is dismissed, killed or runs out
  (you still get the chat line and sound for each as it hits 100%).
- Starring a waiting VNM's zone or piece moves it in front straight away.
- CLEAR ALERT (Settings) clears the waiting ones too.
