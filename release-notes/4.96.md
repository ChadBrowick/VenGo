# VenGo 4.96

GUILD POINTS now looks like the Guild Points section of catseyexi.com/events:

- **Pattern of the day** with its letter in a box, and the reset at JST midnight (15:00 UTC) counting down.
- **OPEN CATSEYEXI.COM/EVENTS** button for the site's page.
- **Select guild**: a button for each of the nine crafting guilds (three across), each with an item icon; a small dot
  marks the guilds you have a rank in. VenGo remembers the one you picked.
- The chosen guild shows **every rank** (Novice to Veteran) with the items it trades ("Trading Initiate items") and
  each item as on the site: "Beetle Mittens +1 (306 pts, max: 8400)". **Your rank is marked in gold.**
- Item names are still links to catseyexi.com/database.
