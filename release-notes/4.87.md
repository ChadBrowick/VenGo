# VenGo 4.87

- New icon: **CHADBROWICK** (Settings > Window > Icon). The emblem from the corner of the window, with the same
  shine sweeping across it and petals drifting past, is now a choice for what VenGo shrinks to.
- A VNM alert puts a pulsing ring round it. See-through logo, Logo size, the corner grip, PREVIEW and "Animate the
  logo" all work for it.
- The Icon choices are now LIGHTNING, AETHER, CHADBROWICK, ORB and NO ICON.
