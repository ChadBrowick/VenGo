**New: three collection tabs** (next to VNM ARMOR)
- **ULTIMATES:** every relic (Spharai to Aegis) with its stage dots and Byne / Whiteshell / Bronzepiece bars, plus Mythic (Alexandrite) and Ergon (H-P Bayld) with their materials. - **ODIOUS CODEX:** each Dynamis zone with its pop items and zone drops, read from your Odious Codex; click a zone to open it.
