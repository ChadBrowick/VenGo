**Download:** VenGo-v4.26.zip below (under Assets).

**Hunter and Summit get their own tabs** (from catseyexi.com/events)
- **HUNTER:** this week's ACE and Crystal Warrior targets, each with zone and points (your mode is marked), and a countdown to Conquest Tally, when they change.
- **SUMMIT:** the current target family with a live countdown, the current buff, and the full list of Summit buffs with the active one marked.
- Updated in the background at load, the moment the Summit's target changes, and every 30 minutes.

Unzip so you have `VanaCore\addons\ashita\vengo\vengo.lua` (or `addons\vanacore\vengo`), then `/addon reload vengo`.
