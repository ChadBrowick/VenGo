# VenGo 4.94

- **When closed** (Settings > Controller): picks what closing VenGo from the controller (LB + Y or B) goes back to:
  - **NOTHING**: VenGo is hidden (the old way, and still the default);
  - **ORB**: the VenGo orb / your chosen icon;
  - **VENTURE BAR**: the one-row venture bar.
- LB + Y opens the full window with the controller again from any of them.
- Closing with the mouse or `/vengo` works as before.
