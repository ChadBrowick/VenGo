# VenGo 4.65

## Japanese / 日本語

- New **EN / JP** switch at the top of the full window (left of the round buttons). JP puts VenGo in Japanese: tabs,
  section names, buttons, settings, hints, tooltips, the VNM pop-up, the venture bar, the dailies tracker and chat lines
  (sent to the game in Shift-JIS). Your choice is saved.
- Names from the game (zones, NMs, items, jobs) stay in English, matching the game data VenGo reads.
- Japanese text uses Noto Sans CJK JP (Medium, Bold for Phantom, Black for titles), cut down to the letters VenGo uses
  (about 155 KB each, in `fonts`, SIL Open Font License).
- The words are in `lang_jp.lua`, so corrections are easy to send in.
