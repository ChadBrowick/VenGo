# VenGo 4.77

The logo settings in Settings > Window now work for both icons.

- **Logo size** (- / + and the corner grip) resizes the orb too: its rings, rim, glow and "VenGo" all scale, from
  40% to 300%.
- **See-through logo** on the orb makes its glass let the game show through; off makes it solid. It no longer follows
  "See-through window".
- **PREVIEW** shows whichever icon is picked.
