# VenGo 4.82

- New **Pulse speed** slider in Settings > GPS: how fast the running light of RUNES, LIGHT and AETHER crosses the
  ground, from 1 to 40 yalms a second (9 to start). All three now use exactly this speed.
- The **Speed** slider above it now only moves the arrows (LINES) and AETHER's small drifting lights.
