**Download:** VenGo-v4.27.zip below (under Assets).

**New: VNM ARMOR tab**
- All five VNM armour sets (Ares, Skadi, Usukane, Marduk, Morrigan), T1-T3, head to feet, with their icons.
- **Green:** you have it (any bag or wardrobe, or stored on Storage Slip 01). **Blue:** you've completed that piece. The header counts how many you have (e.g. 38 / 75).
- **Yellow outline:** drops from one of today's ventures; it **pulses** when that VNM is at 100% or announced. Zones listed twice (Wajaom, Bhaflau, Alzadaal, East Ronfaure [S]) are matched by the VNM's grid square.
- Hover a piece for its drop zones, the ventures dropping it today, and the HNM that also drops it. Hover a set name for its crest, jobs and crest zones.

Set, tier, drop-zone and Storage Slip data from Trove's VNM Armor plugin by Loxley (MIT licence, see `LICENSE-trove.txt`).

Unzip so you have `VanaCore\addons\ashita\vengo\vengo.lua` (or `addons\vanacore\vengo`), then `/addon reload vengo`.
