# VenGo 4.86

- New icon: **AETHER** (Settings > Window > Icon). The chrome "VenGo" with three aether lights circling it: one
  looping the V, one sweeping round the whole word, a small one between. Each has a comet's tail and passes behind
  the word on the far half of its way. A few motes twinkle around it.
- The lights speed up while warping and during a VNM alert, which also puts a pulsing glow round the logo.
- See-through logo, Logo size, the corner grip, PREVIEW and "Animate the logo" all work for it.
- The Icon choices are now LIGHTNING, AETHER, ORB and NO ICON.
