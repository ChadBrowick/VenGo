**Download:** VenGo-v4.56.zip below (under Assets).

- More colours in Settings > Colours:
  - **Accent:** 12 new (Sakura, Mint, Teal, Royal, Cyber, Aurora, Ember, Amber, Lime, Lilac, Blood moon, Silver), 19 in all, in even rows.
  - **Background:** 8 new (Midnight, Wine, Slate, Deep sea, Cocoa, Dusk, Steel, Void), 14 in all.
- **Your own background picture:** CHOOSE PICTURE opens Windows' file picker; the PNG, JPG or BMP you pick is copied into VenGo's `images` folder and shown behind the window, filling it without stretching. **Picture strength** sets how much shows through (lower keeps text easy to read); REMOVE takes it away. Saved in presets too.

Unzip so you have `VanaCore\addons\ashita\vengo\vengo.lua` (and its folders), then `/addon reload vengo`. Or press INSTALL in Settings > Updates.
