# VenGo 5.01

GUILD POINTS:

- **Your guild points** in the chosen craft, at the top next to your rank (read from the game's currencies, the same
  way VenGo reads your sparks), and **what you've earned there today** out of the day's most.
  - Today's count starts from the first total VenGo sees after JST midnight, so points traded in before VenGo was
    running that day aren't counted.
- **A pin** at the right of the craft's name, like the DAILIES pins: a pinned craft shows in the small tracker
  while VenGo is minimized or closed, with your points, today's progress, and a bar for each of today's items: how
  many you carry against how many it still takes to reach today's most. It shows DONE once you've reached it.
