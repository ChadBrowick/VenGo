# VenGo 4.95

- **New tab: GUILD POINTS** (under DAILIES, or `/vengo guild`), from catseyexi.com/events:
  - every crafting guild (Fishing, Woodworking, Smithing, Goldsmithing, Clothcraft, Leathercraft, Bonecraft,
    Alchemy, Cooking) with today's items for **your rank** in that craft;
  - each item's icon, its points each, its stack size, and the most points you can get today;
  - **click an item's name** to look it up on catseyexi.com/database, as on the DAILIES tab;
  - click a guild to see every rank's items (and again for just yours);
  - a countdown to the JP-midnight change, and VenGo fetches the new items just after it.
- Needs "Update from catseyexi.com/events" on in Settings (it's fetched with Hunter and Summit).
- In Japanese too: guild and rank names as in the Japanese client.
