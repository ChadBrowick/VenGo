# VenGo 4.73

- **New logo.** Shrunk, VenGo is now its lightning logo instead of the round orb: bolts gather into "VenGo", which
  then holds and strikes again every 9 seconds, or when you point at it. While warping or during a VNM alert it
  strikes every 3 seconds, with a pulsing glow for an alert.
- Click it to open VenGo and drag it to move it, as before. "Animate the logo" off in Settings keeps it still.
