**Download:** VenGo-v4.50.zip below (under Assets).

- DAILIES: every daily card has a **pin** (left of its status pill). Pin the ones you're working on.
- While VenGo is minimized (or closed), a small **DAILIES tracker** shows each pinned daily:
  - its name and zone (or TURN IN / DONE);
  - its objective, in smaller text;
  - its kill bar, and a bar for each item you need, with how many you carry;
  - the time to the JP-midnight reset at the top.
- Drag the tracker anywhere; click a pin on it to unpin. It hides while the full VenGo window is open. Pins are saved (and kept in presets).

Unzip so you have `VanaCore\addons\ashita\vengo\vengo.lua` (or `addons\vanacore\vengo`), then `/addon reload vengo`. Or press INSTALL in Settings > Updates.
