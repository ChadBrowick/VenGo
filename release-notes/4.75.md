# VenGo 4.75

New in Settings > Window, for the lightning logo:

- **See-through logo**: on (the default), nothing is drawn behind the logo. Off puts a dark cloud behind it, easier
  to read over bright ground. This is separate from "See-through window".
- **Logo size** with **-** and **+**: 10% a press, from 40% to 300%. Dragging the logo's corner still works.
- **PREVIEW**: shows the logo beside the open window, so you can see the size and background as you change them.
  Click the logo or the button again to hide it.
