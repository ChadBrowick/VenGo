# VenGo 4.88

How the LIGHTNING and AETHER icons show that a VNM is up. The blinking ring round them is gone.

- **AETHER:** its three lights swell, glow brighter and blink, each with a wide flare that comes and goes.
- **LIGHTNING:** the bolts keep striking one after another instead of settling on the word, and the whole logo is
  brighter, with a flickering glow of itself.
- With "Animate the logo" off, the lightning stays on the finished word but still brightens and flickers, and the
  aether lights stay in place but still blink.
- The ORB and CHADBROWICK icons keep their pulsing ring.
