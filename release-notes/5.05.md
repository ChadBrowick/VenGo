# VenGo 5.05

- **Fixed: Lumoria didn't show which stage your Homam and Nashira pieces are at.** The CatsEyeXI server's answer
  can report an augmented piece as plain (as it did for an augmented Homam Zucchetto: no stage dots, "Base (no
  augments)"). VenGo now also reads the augments on the pieces in your bags, inventory, Mog House and wardrobes, and
  shows the higher of the two: the stage dots, the piece's augments in its tooltip, and the set's count.
- Each stage is told from the augments themselves, so it's right however many copies you carry (the best one
  counts). Pieces kept with the Squire still come from the server.
