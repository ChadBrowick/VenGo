**Download:** VenGo-v4.35.zip below (under Assets).

**Fixed: the steady FPS loss while VenGo is loaded**
- To put the arrows on the ground, VenGo reads the game's camera from its draw calls. It was listening to every draw call (thousands a frame) all the time, even with no arrows showing, and just being called that often cost FPS.
- Now VenGo listens to draw calls only on the frames it reads the camera, and only while arrows are showing. Outside a venture's zone, with the trail off, or after STOP, it isn't called on draw calls at all.
- While arrows are showing, the camera-read setting (Settings > Performance > Camera: read it every N frames) and Lightweight mode lower the cost further.

Unzip so you have `VanaCore\addons\ashita\vengo\vengo.lua` (or `addons\vanacore\vengo`), then `/addon reload vengo`.
