# VenGo 4.81

- GPS (RUNES, LIGHT, AETHER): the running light now crosses the ground at one steady speed. Before, its position was
  worked out from the path's length, so it sped up, slowed down and jumped as you walked closer. It now keeps its own
  place and moves on by the time since the last frame, whatever you do.
- AETHER's drifting motes are counted back from the VNM, so walking toward it no longer speeds them up.
