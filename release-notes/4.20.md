**Download:** VenGo-v4.20.zip below (under Assets).

**New: arrows on the ground to the Venture Seal**
- In a venture's zone, glowing arrows run along the ground from your feet toward the VNM, with a flag showing the name and distance.
- They head for the VNM's grid square, then switch to the Venture Seal itself once it's loaded (about 50 yalms), and remember where each zone's Seal is for next time.
- In the venture's zone, GO / STOP turns the arrows on and off.
- Settings → Trail to the VNM: on/off, glow, style (Lines, Neon Bands, Solid Neon), size (Small, Medium, Large) and colour (8 colours or Neon Mix).

**Also**
- `/vengo where` shows your grid square and the trail's target, to check positions.

Unzip so you have `VanaCore\addons\ashita\vengo\vengo.lua` (or `addons\vanacore\vengo`), then `/addon reload vengo`.
