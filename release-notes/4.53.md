**Download:** VenGo-v4.53.zip below (under Assets).

- Two new switches in Settings > VNM Alerts (both on by default):
  - **Chat notifications:** off stops VenGo's VNM chat lines (the VNM ALERT line, "pops in about a minute", "no longer at 100%", the 90% heads-up and starred-VNM warnings). They still show in VenGo's status line.
  - **Pop-up notification:** off stops the VNM INCOMING window. The alert still shows in VenGo, the orb still glows, and the sound still plays if it's on. LB + A / LB + B go to the game as usual while it's off.
- Both are saved in presets.

Unzip so you have `VanaCore\addons\ashita\vengo\vengo.lua` (or `addons\vanacore\vengo`), then `/addon reload vengo`. Or press INSTALL in Settings > Updates.
