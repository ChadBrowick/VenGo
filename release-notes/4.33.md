**Download:** VenGo-v4.33.zip below (under Assets).

**New: stars**
- A star beside every venture's GO button watches that zone. On the VNM ARMOR tab, click any piece to star it: every venture that drops it is watched.
- Watched VNMs get a chat line and a sound at 90%, at 100% and a minute before the pop. Settings > Starred turns the chat lines and sound on or off and clears stars.

**New: controller support**
- `/vengo pad` (put it in a macro) or Settings > Controller gives VenGo the controller: the d-pad moves between buttons and tabs, A presses. Back / Select gives it back to the game.

**New: updates from GitHub**
- VenGo checks this repository for new releases (30 seconds after loading, then every 6 hours) and shows a notice on the Ventures tab.
- INSTALL (or `/vengo upgrade`) downloads the release's zip from github.com/ChadBrowick/VenGo only, unpacks it over the vengo folder, and tells you to `/addon reload vengo`. Your settings, stars and presets aren't in the zip, so they stay.

Unzip so you have `VanaCore\addons\ashita\vengo\vengo.lua` (or `addons\vanacore\vengo`), then `/addon reload vengo`.
