**Download:** VenGo-v4.44.zip below (under Assets).

- VNM INCOMING alert from the controller: hold **LB** and press **A** for GO (STOP or cancel when that's what the button shows), or **LB + B** to dismiss it.
  - Works without giving VenGo the controller; A and B go to the game as usual whenever the alert isn't showing or LB isn't held.
  - Once VenGo has seen your controller, the alert shows the two shortcuts under its buttons.

Unzip so you have `VanaCore\addons\ashita\vengo\vengo.lua` (or `addons\vanacore\vengo`), then `/addon reload vengo`. Or press INSTALL in Settings > Updates.
