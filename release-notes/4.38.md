**Download:** VenGo-v4.38.zip below (under Assets).

- New **DAILIES** tab under SUMMIT (or `/vengo dailies`): the goblin dailies in Lower Jeuno (Fishstix, Murdox, Mistrix, Saltlix, Beetrix), Goldilox's dice reward, and Palalumin's three Sea dailies.
  - Talk to each NPC and VenGo reads the day's task from chat: state (not started / in progress / turn in / done), zone, kill bars, and how many of the wanted items you carry.
  - Click a mob or item name in violet to look it up on the catseyexi.com database.
  - Resets at JP midnight with a countdown; kept per character. CLEAR TODAY wipes the day if something was misread.
- Ported from Goldilox by Dewin (MIT licence, `LICENSE-goldilox.txt`); added to Special Thanks.

Unzip so you have `VanaCore\addons\ashita\vengo\vengo.lua` (or `addons\vanacore\vengo`), then `/addon reload vengo`. Or press INSTALL in Settings > Updates.
